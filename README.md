# FYP Research & Proposal Preparation

## Overview

This repository serves as the central workspace for research, preliminary system design, and proposal preparation for our Final Year Project (FYP).

It maintains a record of our research activities, technical investigations, design iterations, and progress as we work toward finalizing the project proposal.

## Current Project Direction

We are exploring a machine-learning-based platform for microservices monitoring, anomaly detection, and root-cause analysis.

The current direction involves investigating distributed-system telemetry, service dependency graphs, and machine-learning or graph-based approaches for identifying potential causes of service failures.

The exact scope, architecture, algorithms, datasets, and technology stack are not finalized and will be refined through research and feasibility analysis.

## Current Phase

**Phase:** Research and Proposal Preparation

Our current priorities are:

- Reviewing relevant research papers and existing solutions.
- Identifying the problem statement and research gap.
- Investigating suitable datasets and evaluation methods.
- Exploring potential machine-learning and graph-based approaches.
- Developing preliminary system architecture and workflow diagrams.
- Evaluating technologies and defining the project scope.
- Preparing the FYP proposal.
