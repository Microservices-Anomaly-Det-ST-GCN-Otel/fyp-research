# FYP Research and Proposal Preparation

## Overview

This repository maintains the research, planning, preliminary system design, and proposal preparation for our Final Year Project.

## Current Project Direction

We are investigating a machine-learning-based platform for microservices monitoring, anomaly detection, and root-cause analysis.

The exact scope, architecture, algorithms, datasets, and technology choices are still under evaluation and will be refined through literature review and feasibility analysis.

## Current Phase

**Phase:** Research and Proposal Preparation

Current activities:
- Reviewing related research papers.
- Identifying the problem statement and research gap.
- Evaluating datasets and potential ML approaches.
- Developing preliminary system architecture and workflow diagrams.
- Comparing technologies and defining project scope.

## Repository Structure

- `research/` — Research papers, summaries, and technology comparisons.
- `design/` — Preliminary architecture diagrams and system flows.
- `proposal/` — Proposal drafts and related materials.
- `meetings/` — Supervisor feedback and meeting notes.
- `progress/` — Periodic progress updates.

## Working Principles

- Proposed technologies are not considered finalized until supported by research and feasibility analysis.
- Significant changes to the architecture and project scope should be recorded with their rationale.
- Research findings should reference their original sources.
- Progress updates should reflect work actually completed.

## Team Workflow

Research tasks and action items are tracked through GitHub Issues. Research artifacts and design changes are maintained through version control.

## Current Status

Initial project direction identified. Research, feasibility analysis, and proposal preparation are in progress.
